ABOUT GPIWL

Empowering Women Through Technical Education

Government Polytechnic Institute Women Landhi (GPIWL) is committed to providing quality technical and professional education to young women in a supportive and inclusive environment. Our mission is to equip students with the skills and knowledge needed to excel in their careers and contribute positively to society.

  • Experienced Faculty Learn from highly qualified and experienced teachers.
  • Modern Labs & Workshops Hands-on training with state-of-the-art equipment.
  • Career Opportunities Preparing students for successful professional careers.
